隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

更新日期 / Last Updated：2026-09-18

1. 概述 / Overview

本网站为免费在线工具集合站点，旨在为用户提供各类浏览器在线工具服务。本隐私政策用于说明本站如何收集、使用、存储与保护用户访问数据，同时说明第三方广告与统计服务的数据处理规则，适用于所有访问本站的用户。

This website is a free online tool platform that provides various browser‑based tool services. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect user access data, as well as the data processing rules of third‑party advertising and analytics services. This policy applies to all visitors of this website.

2. 信息收集 / Information Collection

本站不主动收集、不保存用户个人隐私信息，无需用户注册、登录、填写手机号、邮箱等个人资料。

We do not actively collect or store any personal private information. No registration, login, phone number or email is required to use our tools.

在用户访问过程中，会自动被动获取少量访问日志信息，包括：访问设备类型、浏览器类型、访问页面、访问时间、IP属地（非精准IP）。该数据仅用于网站访问统计、故障排查、流量分析。

We automatically collect anonymous access logs during user visits, including device type, browser type, visited pages, access time, and regional IP information (non‑precise IP). These data are only used for website statistics, troubleshooting and traffic analysis.

3. Cookie 与本地缓存 / Cookies & Local Storage

本站会使用 Cookie 及本地缓存技术优化访问体验、统计网站流量。您可以通过浏览器设置随时禁止、清除 Cookie，关闭 Cookie 不会影响本站基础工具使用。

This website uses cookies and local storage to optimize user experience and analyze website traffic. You may disable or clear cookies via your browser settings at any time. Disabling cookies will not affect the basic functions of our tools.

4. 第三方服务（广告与统计）/ Third‑Party Services

本站接入 Google AdSense 广告服务，用于展示互联网广告、维持站点运营。

This website uses Google AdSense to display online advertisements and support website operation.

Google 可能会通过 Cookie 收集匿名访问数据，用于展示个性化广告、统计广告效果。相关数据受 Google 隐私政策约束。

Google may collect anonymous data via cookies for personalized advertising and ad performance statistics. All related data processing is subject to Google’s Privacy Policy.

本站不会出售、出租任何用户数据。

We will never sell or rent any user data to third parties.

5. 数据安全 / Data Security

本站为静态网页站点，无后端数据存储、无用户数据库，不存在用户隐私数据泄露风险。我们采用 HTTPS 加密访问，保障浏览安全。

This is a static website with no backend database and no user data storage. There is no risk of personal data leakage. We use HTTPS encryption to ensure secure browsing.

6. 未成年人保护 / Children's Privacy

本站面向所有用户开放，不针对未成年人提供特殊服务，不会主动收集未成年人隐私信息。

This website is open to all users. We do not provide services targeted at minors and do not actively collect any personal information from children.

7. 用户权利 / User Rights

用户可随时通过浏览器清除本地缓存、Cookie；可自由选择是否浏览本站内容。如对本站隐私规则有疑问，可通过页面公开渠道联系我们。

Users may clear local storage and cookies through browser settings at any time. You may choose to stop browsing this website freely. If you have questions about this privacy policy, you can contact us through public channels.

8. 政策更新 / Policy Updates

本站保留随时更新本隐私政策的权利，更新后将在页面公示，无需单独通知用户。持续访问本站即视为同意最新隐私政策。

We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy at any time. Updates will be posted on this page without individual notification. Continued use of the website constitutes acceptance of the latest policy.
